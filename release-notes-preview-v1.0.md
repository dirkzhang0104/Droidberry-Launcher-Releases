# Droidberry Launcher preview-v1.0

Droidberry Launcher turns your Android device into a clean, BlackBerry-inspired home screen built for quick access, easy navigation, and a more organized inbox.

## What you get

### 📬 A smarter inbox for all notifications
- **One inbox for everything.** All notifications are gathered into one place so you can browse them in one simple, chronological stream.
- **Easy filtering.** Narrow the inbox by type, such as messages, email, social apps, or calls.
- **Unread badge at a glance.** The Hub shows an unread badge on the home screen when something new arrives.
- **Open or clear with one tap.** Tap any notification to open the app it came from, then remove items one by one or in bulk.
- **Reply without leaving.** Send a reply directly from the Hub.
- **Fast exit.** Swipe down inside the Hub to close it quickly.

### 📱 A launcher that adapts to your device
- **Built for more than one screen shape.** Droidberry works on phones, tablets, and foldable devices.
- **Adapts as you fold and unfold.** It automatically changes its layout for folded, open, book, and tabletop postures.
- **Bottom bar when folded.** On folded screens, you get a bottom navigation bar.
- **Side rail when unfolded.** On unfolded screens, you get a side navigation rail for a more spacious layout.
- **Grid size follows screen width.** The app drawer automatically adjusts how many columns it shows.
- **More room on larger screens.** The dock can hold more apps on larger inner screens.

### 🏠 A home screen that stays useful at a glance
- **Clock, date, and day.** See the day, time, and date right on the home screen.
- **Weather at a glance.** View current weather using your device location, with automatic temperature units based on your locale.
- **Battery always visible.** Keep the battery indicator next to the clock.
- **Shortcuts where you need them.** Jump straight to the Hub or app drawer with built-in shortcuts.
- **Widgets on the home screen.** Add Android widgets, move them around, resize them, or remove them when you no longer need them.
- **Automatic layout protection.** The layout lock protects your setup when the screen turns off so items do not shift by accident.
- **Optional Google side panel.** Swipe right to open the Google app as a side panel if you want a Google Feed style experience.

### 🔎 Find and launch apps faster
- **Searchable app drawer.** Browse all launchable apps in a searchable, adaptive app drawer.
- **Work profile support.** Work apps are shown clearly and launch in the correct work context.
- **Tag-based organization.** Use tags to organize apps and filter the drawer.
- **Dedicated search screen.** Open a dedicated app search screen from a gesture or shortcut.
- **App actions on long press.** Long-press an app to pin it to the dock, change its icon, or open app info.

### 🧰 A dock that fits your routine
- **Always available.** Keep a permanent dock at the bottom of the screen if you want quick access to your favorite apps.
- **Drag to reorder.** Rearrange dock items by dragging them into place.
- **Fits the screen you are using.** Use up to 5 apps on the outer screen or slab-style phone layouts, and up to 12 apps on the inner screen.
- **Easy to toggle.** Turn the dock on or off in Settings.

### 👆 Gestures you can make your own
- **Custom actions.** Set swipe up, swipe down, and double tap to open the app drawer, open the Hub, open search, expand notifications, lock the screen, or do nothing.
- **Ready out of the box.** The default setup is tuned for quick use: swipe up opens the app drawer, swipe down opens notifications, and double tap locks the screen.

### 🎨 Icons and visual control
- **Icon packs supported.** Droidberry supports standard icon packs from popular launchers and themes.
- **Per-app overrides.** You can override the icon for any individual app without changing the whole pack.
- **Easy rollback.** You can always revert an icon override back to the default.
- **Size options.** Adjust app icon size to 100%, 120%, or 150%.

### 💾 Backup and restore
- **One-file export.** Export your launcher setup to a single JSON file.
- **Store it anywhere.** Save that file on your device or in cloud storage.
- **Restore later.** Bring your setup back from a previously exported file.

### ℹ️ Other useful details
- **Edge-to-edge design.** The launcher runs edge-to-edge with transparent system bars.
- **Default launcher ready.** It can be set as your default home app.
- **Optional accessibility service.** An accessibility service powers the swipe-down notification gesture and the lock-screen gesture, and it is fully optional.
- **Home button behavior.** Pressing the Home button while you are inside Droidberry always brings you back to the main home screen.

## In short

preview-v1.0 focuses on a fast home screen, a powerful unified inbox, foldable-friendly layouts, and a setup that feels more personal without getting in your way.
